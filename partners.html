Why partner

Our model only works if yours does.

Guests with intent

Matches expire, so there's a reason to lock in a time rather than let it sit. And every date on the calendar is two people who already agreed when they're showing up — not a coupon someone might get around to using.

Your slow hours

Set the windows you want filled — weeknight evenings, early seatings — and String routes dates to exactly those times.

Repeat business

A great first date becomes "their spot." Couples come back on their own time, and bring friends with them.

Brand presence

Partner venues carry String presence in-house — including matchbooks on the table — placement that lands exactly where your guests already are.

How it works for you

Free listing. Paid placement. No cut of your bill.

We charge for placement, not performance. There's no commission on what your table spends, ever — String makes money from venues choosing to be seen, not from your check.
