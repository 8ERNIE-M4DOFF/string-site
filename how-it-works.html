Match to date, by design

Every mechanic in String exists to close the distance between "you two connected" and "you two met." Here's the path.

1

Connect

Matches are capped at ten. When you connect with someone, they have your real attention — not a place in an infinite queue.

2

The clock starts

Every match expires in fourteen days. The deadline isn't pressure — it's permission to skip weeks of small talk and make a plan.

3

String books the date

From the moment you connect, String proposes a confirmed date at a partner venue near you both — the where and when, handled.

4

Walk through the door

Confirm the venue and time in a tap. That's where our job ends and the actual date begins.

Why the constraints

The limits are the feature.

  • A 10-match cap makes every match a decision, not a collection.
  • A 14-day expiry creates momentum — matches become dates or make room.
  • A confirmed venue removes the "we should get drinks sometime" limbo.
  • Partner venues mean every first date lands at a real, vetted Chicago spot.
