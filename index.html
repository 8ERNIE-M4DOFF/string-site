The idea

Matches only matter if they become dates.

Capped at 10

Active matches are limited to ten at a time. Every connection has your attention, because none of them are filler in an endless deck.

14 days, then gone

Each match expires in fourteen days. A match isn't something to collect and forget — it's something to act on while it's live.

A confirmed venue

The moment two people connect, String drives the pair toward a real date at a partner venue near you both. We handle the where and when.

The model

We earn from venues, not from you.

Our revenue comes from venue partnerships, not subscriptions — and not a cut of your date. Venues pay to be featured in recommendations; you never pay us anything, and we never take a percentage of what you spend. Our incentives point toward getting you an actual date, not keeping you swiping.

Safety

Built with real dates in mind.

Meeting someone new should feel good, not risky. String includes safety tools most dating apps treat as an afterthought.

Check-in timer

Set a deadline before your date. If you don't confirm you're safe, we text the emergency contact you chose.

Location, shared

Your emergency contact gets a link to where you are, for as long as you choose to share it.

Verified accounts

Every account is phone-verified before it can match with anyone.

Reports that get reviewed

Reporting and blocking are built in. Repeated reports freeze an account for review.
