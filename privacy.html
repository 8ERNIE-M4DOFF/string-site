2. What we collect

4. Text messages (SMS)

String sends SMS in two situations.

Sign-in codes. When you sign in, we send a one-time passcode to the phone number you provide.

Safety alerts. If you start a check-in timer before a date and do not confirm you are safe before it expires, we send a text message to the emergency contact you chose.

Message frequency. Sign-in codes are sent only when you request one. Safety alerts are sent only when a check-in timer you started expires without confirmation. Neither is recurring, and String does not send marketing or promotional text messages.

Message and data rates may apply.

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers with third parties for marketing purposes. Phone numbers are shared only with the service providers listed in Section 7 who deliver messages on our behalf, and only for that purpose.

To stop receiving safety alerts, remove your emergency contact in the app's safety settings. You may also reply STOP to any message from us. Replying STOP will prevent us from sending you further messages, including safety alerts.

6. Your emergency contact — someone else's information

When you add an emergency contact, you give us the name and phone number of a person who is not a String user and has not agreed to anything with us.

We take that seriously. Specifically:

  • We store only the name and number you enter. We do not look them up, enrich the record, or contact them for any other reason.
  • We message them only if a check-in timer you started expires without your confirmation.
  • The message tells them your first name, that a check-in you set has expired, and asks them to check on you. It may include a link showing your last shared location.
  • We never use their number for marketing, and we never share it with anyone except the provider that delivers the message.
  • You can remove them at any time in the app's safety settings, which deletes their information.

Please tell the person you have listed them. They will not otherwise know until they receive a message.

7. How long we keep things

  • Account data: while your account exists.
  • Precise location history: deleted 30 days after it is created.
  • Messages and matches: while your account exists, or until the match ends.
  • Emergency contact: until you remove it or delete your account.
  • Reports and safety records: retained after account deletion where necessary to keep other users safe. See Section 9.

8. Your choices

  • See your data: contact support@stringdatingapp.com and we will provide what we hold about you.
  • Correct it: most of it is editable in the app; for the rest, contact us.
  • Delete your account: available in the app under Profile → Delete Account. This is permanent.
  • Turn off notifications: in your device settings or the app.
  • Remove your emergency contact: in the app's safety settings.
  • Location: you can change the location you gave us in the app.

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the CCPA, including the right to know what we collect, to request deletion, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. We do not sell personal information as that term is defined by the CCPA.

9. What survives account deletion

Deleting your account removes your profile, photos, messages, matches, and emergency contact.

Two things do not go away:

Safety reports filed about you by other people. These are the other person's account of their own experience, and we keep them so that a record of a safety concern cannot be erased by the person it concerns.

A record that an account was banned. If your account was banned for a safety reason, we retain a one-way cryptographic hash of the phone number so that the ban cannot be trivially undone by deleting and re-registering. This hash cannot be reversed to recover your number by ordinary means, but we want to be clear that it is derived from it. We retain it only for accounts banned for safety reasons.

If you believe either applies to you in error, contact support@stringdatingapp.com.

10. Security

We use encryption in transit, store credentials with a managed authentication provider, and restrict internal access to production data. No system is perfectly secure, and we will not claim otherwise.

11. Age

String is for people 18 and older. We ask for your date of birth at signup and do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to use the app. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we delete it.

12. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that materially affects you, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
